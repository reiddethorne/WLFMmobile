# WLFM store submission materials

This directory is the version-controlled source for App Store and Google Play
listing materials. It is not bundled into the mobile application. Copy the
approved text and upload the approved images to App Store Connect and Google
Play Console when the organization accounts are ready.

Do not submit any file while it still contains `TODO(RELEASE)` markers.

## Contents

- `metadata/en-US.md` contains the proposed US English listing text and the
  store questionnaire decisions that still need an owner.
- `privacy-policy.md` is a draft for institutional review and publication on a
  public WLFM or Lawrence University webpage.
- `review-notes.md` contains notes for Apple App Review and Google Play review.
- `ios/README.md` describes the iPhone and iPad assets to capture.
- `android/README.md` describes the Google Play graphics to prepare.

## Workflow

1. Replace every `TODO(RELEASE)` marker with an approved answer.
2. Have the privacy policy reviewed by the responsible Lawrence University
   office and publish it as a normal public webpage, not a PDF.
3. Capture screenshots from the final store-signed release candidate with no
   personal information, debugging UI, or unrelated notifications visible.
4. Keep original screenshots and editable artwork in this directory. Export
   final PNG or JPEG files into the platform directories.
5. Upload the listing text and graphics in each store console.
6. Recheck the privacy and content declarations whenever app behavior or a
   third-party integration changes.
