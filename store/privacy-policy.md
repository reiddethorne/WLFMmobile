# Privacy Policy for the WLFM Mobile App

**Effective date:** `TODO(RELEASE): Month Day, Year`

The WLFM mobile application (the **App**) is published by `TODO(RELEASE): legal
publisher name` on behalf of WLFM, Lawrence University's student radio station.
This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## Information you provide

The App does not require you to create an account or submit your name, email
address, payment information, location, contacts, photos, or recordings. If you
choose to contact WLFM through a link in the App, your browser opens the WLFM
website and information you submit there is handled by that website.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information locally so that your choices persist:

- appearance and schedule-time preferences;
- favorite-show and show-reminder settings;
- scheduled local notification details; and
- an optional diagnostics-display preference.

This information is used only to provide App features. WLFM does not receive it
through a separate application server. You can remove it by changing the
relevant settings, cancelling reminders, clearing the App's storage where your
device permits, or uninstalling the App. Your operating system may retain some
device-managed information according to its own backup and notification rules.

## Notifications

If you enable show reminders, the App asks for notification permission and
schedules reminders locally on your device. The App does not register the
device for WLFM marketing push notifications and does not send WLFM a push
notification token. You can disable notifications in the App or in your device
settings.

## Network services and automatically transmitted information

The App needs an internet connection for live audio, current song information,
the station schedule, album artwork, and station stories. To provide those
features, it makes direct HTTPS requests to:

- Live365 for the WLFM stream and current or recently played track information;
- Google Calendar for WLFM's public program schedule;
- the WLFM WordPress website for published station stories; and
- the content-delivery or image hosts referenced by those services.

Like most internet services, these providers can receive routine technical
information when your device connects, such as your IP address, the time of the
request, the requested resource, and basic device or application request
information. Their handling and retention of that information is governed by
their own policies. WLFM does not operate a separate App server and does not
receive or maintain those service logs through the App.

The App may also open external webpages or media links in your system browser.
Once opened, those destinations are governed by their own privacy practices.

## Sharing and sale of information

WLFM does not sell listener personal information. The App sends requests to the
services described above only as needed to provide requested features. The App
does not currently include a third-party analytics SDK or an in-app advertising
SDK.

## Data retention

WLFM does not maintain a separate account or server-side profile for App users.
Locally stored preferences and reminder settings remain on your device until
you change or remove them as described above. Third-party network services set
their own retention periods for any routine service logs they maintain.

## Security

The App uses encrypted HTTPS connections for its supported network requests.
No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but the
App is designed to limit the information it handles to what is needed for its
features.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience of teen and adult radio listeners
and is not designed specifically for children. Live radio programming may
contain explicit language or mature themes. The App does not knowingly request
personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App, its services, or applicable
requirements change. The published policy will show its effective date.
